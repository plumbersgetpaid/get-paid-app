00 · The big picture

What PatchUp does

PatchUp is a job-management app for UK tradespeople — plumbers, electricians, builders. It runs on your phone and handles everything between winning a job and getting paid for it. The whole product is built around one loop.

01
Quote
Send a priced quote
02
Accept
Customer says yes
03
Book in
Put it on the calendar
04
Complete
Mark done, add photos
05
Invoice
Goes out automatically
06
Chase
Auto-chased until paid
07
Paid
Money in

Everything else — clients, calendar, team, settings — supports that loop. If you film nothing else, film the loop (Section 04). The one line to open a video with: "You finish the job. PatchUp handles getting paid."

01 · Before you film

Two kinds of user (this changes what's on screen)

The app looks different depending on who's logged in, so decide which view a shot is showing:

Owner / Manager

sees everything

The boss. Sees money, all jobs, all clients, invoices, settings and billing. Film as an owner when you want the full app. (Owner and Manager are near-identical — an owner just can't be demoted or removed.)

Subcontractor ("your lads")

only their jobs

Sees only the jobs assigned to them — no money, no client list, no settings. The owner can switch on up to six extra abilities one at a time (Section 12). Film this view for the stripped-back "just my jobs" experience.

Throughout this guide, anything marked owner/manager is hidden for a plain subcontractor.

02 · Getting in

Signup, the free trial, and login

Sign up for a free trial

/signup
What it's for
A new tradesperson starting their 14-day free trial.
On screen
The logo, heading "Set up your business," and "Free for 14 days. No card needed." Then: Business name ("This goes on your quotes and invoices"), Your name, Email, Password, Confirm password, and "How many of you are there?" — number pills (1–10) with a live price underneath.
To do it
Fill it in → tap "Start 14-day free trial."
What happens
The account is created and you land on Today. No card is asked for.
Pricing to show: £19 a month covers one person; every extra person is £8. So two people = £27, three = £35.

Log in

/login
On screen
Logo, Email, Password, a "Forgot password?" link, and "Log in." Below: "New here? Start a free trial."
What happens
Lands on Today.

Forgot / reset password

/forgot-password
On screen
One Email box and "Send reset link." Whatever you type, it always says "If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way" — so it can't be used to discover who has an account. The emailed link opens /reset-password: New password + Confirm → "Set new password."

03 · Orientation

Finding your way around

These appear on every logged-in screen — film them once, early.

The bottom navigation bar

Fixed to the bottom, four tabs left to right: Today (home), Work (quotes/jobs/invoices/reminders), Calendar, and Clients. The active tab has a silver underline. note Clients only appears if the user can see the client database — hidden for basic subcontractors.

The "+" button (create anything)

A round silver + floats in the middle above the nav. Tapping it dims the screen and opens a menu: New quote, Quick book a job, Personal reminder, Recurring job (each shown only if that user is allowed it). The fastest way to start almost anything — a good recurring shot.

"Install PatchUp" & "Never miss a job"

Two one-time cards appear above the nav. Install PatchUp adds the app to the home screen (Android shows the native prompt; iPhone shows "Tap Share → Add to Home Screen"). Never miss a job turns on notifications for a nudge an hour before each job. Both are dismissible.

04 · The heart of the app

The core loop, step by step

Film this in order — it's the film. Quote → accept → book → complete → auto-invoice → chase → paid.

4.1 · Add a client

/clients/new
What it's for
Adding a customer. You usually don't need to — a client is created automatically the first time you quote or book them. This is for adding one by hand.
On screen
Name (required), Phone, Email, Address → Save client.

4.2 · Create and send a quote

/jobs/newquote permission
What it's for
Sending a customer a priced quote — the top of the loop.
On screen
  • Customer name (required), Phone, Email.
  • A "Record voice note" mic — speak the details and the AI fills the fields (Section 18).
  • Job type (with a wand button that tidies the wording with AI), Quoted amount (£) (required), Job location.
  • A collapsible "Proposed date & duration" — set a date here and accepting the quote books it automatically.
  • Assign to — tick which team members it's for.
To do it
Fill it in → Send quote → a confirmation card shows the customer, job, quote total and where it will be emailed → Confirm & send quote.
What happens
The customer is emailed a professional quote, the job appears under Work → Quotes (amber), and you return to Today.

4.3 · The customer replies

Work → Quotes
On screen
Each waiting quote has three buttons:
  • Accept quote — moves it into Jobs (in progress). If a date was proposed it's already booked; otherwise book it next.
  • Chase quote — sends a friendly "following up on your quote" email. After ~3 days a quote is flagged "worth chasing."
  • Decline / lost job — marks it lost (asks to confirm first).

4.4 · Book the job in

/jobs/schedule/[job]reschedule permission
What it's for
Putting an accepted job on the calendar with a real date and time.
On screen
A "Book by voice" mic; Start date, Start time, Expected duration (number + unit); location; an "Include weekends" tick; and "Let the client know" (an email tick that sends a booking confirmation). If it clashes with another job, an amber warning shows and the button becomes "Book anyway."
To do it
Set date/time → Confirm booking.
What happens
The job shows on the calendar and on Today, and (if ticked) the customer gets a confirmation email.
Shortcut — Quick book (/calendar/quick-book): book a job on the spot with no quote first (e.g. standing in the customer's kitchen). Same fields plus name/price; creates the booked job in one go. Invoice it properly later.
Every send gets a look-first card: quotes, quick-book and recurring jobs all show a review card before saving — customer, price, date, and whether the customer will be emailed. Untick "Email" (or leave email blank) and the card warns in amber that the customer won't be notified, with "Go back" to change it. Nothing customer-facing happens silently.

4.5 · Do the job, then mark it done

/jobs/complete/[job]invoice permission
What it's for
Finishing a job — this is where the invoice is born.
On screen
  • A summary; any important notes show in an amber banner.
  • owner/manager Final invoice amount (pre-filled with the quote — change it if the work changed), Payment due date (defaults +14 days), Payment link (adds a "Pay now" button).
  • Completion note with an "Enhance note with AI" button.
  • Before / after photos — two camera buttons; these become a permanent part of the invoice PDF.
To do it
"Mark done & send invoice" → a confirmation card shows the customer, invoice total, due date and photo count, with "it can't be unsent" (and flags if the amount differs from the quote) → "Confirm & send invoice."
What happens
The job is completed, an invoice PDF (with your logo and any photos) is generated and emailed to the customer automatically.
Offline moment worth filming: with no signal, the whole completion — photos included — saves on the phone and a black "Saved on this phone ✓" panel appears. It sends itself the moment you're back online (Section 17).

4.6 · The invoice

/invoices/[invoice]
On screen
The rendered invoice (number, customer, line item, due date, status), with Download PDF, a payment link editor, and a CSV download for the accountant.

4.7 · Get paid

Work → Invoices
On screen
Each unpaid invoice has Chase now (emails the customer the chaser with the PDF) and Mark as paid (records it paid, and triggers the "please leave a review" email if a Google review link is set).
Automatic
You don't have to remember — PatchUp emails overdue customers by itself at 3, 7 and 14 days overdue, in wording you control.

05 · Home

Today — the dashboard

The / screen you land on after login: an at-a-glance "what do I need to deal with." Cards, top to bottom:

Trial banner
owner/manager In the last week of the trial: e.g. "3 DAYS LEFT" with "Set up payment →." Turns urgent (amber) at ≤3 days.
Today's schedule
Time-ordered jobs and reminders (or "TBC"). Tapping a job goes to complete it, or to book it if it still needs a time. Empty: "Nothing booked in for today."
Action needed
A self-building to-do: quotes needing a reply, jobs running late, jobs needing a time, jobs to book, invoices due/overdue — each taps through. If nothing's pending, an all-clear card.
Outstanding payments
owner/manager A big £ total awaiting payment, any overdue amount in red, "View invoices →."

06 · The control centre

Work — quotes, jobs, invoices, reminders

The working list of everything on the go (/work), across four tabs:

Quotes
owner/manager Sub-tabs "Waiting response" / "Already chased." Amber cards with Accept, Chase, Decline (Section 4.3).
Jobs
Sub-tabs "Today & overdue / Upcoming / Unscheduled / Completed." Blue cards (red if late, green when done), a "View job" button (amber "!" if there's an important note), and an inline assign/share control for owner/manager.
Invoices
invoice permission Sub-tabs "Overdue / Awaiting / Paid." Red cards with Chase now and Mark as paid.
Reminders
Your personal calendar reminders (not jobs), with "+ Reminder" and Upcoming/Past sub-tabs.

07 · By date

Calendar

Everything by date in one place (/calendar): + Quick book and + Reminder buttons, Today / Week / Month views, and a ‹ › stepper. Day-by-day cards, colour-coded:

BlueBooked job (red if overdue)
RedInvoice payment due
PurplePersonal reminder
GoldProjected recurring job

Reminders (/calendar/reminder/new): title, notes, date/time, duration, an "Include weekends" tick, and — for owner/manager — an "Also share with" list. Can be created by voice; opening one lets you edit or delete it.

08 · Inside a job

Job details, notes & photos

Job details

/jobs/view/[job]

Contact info (tap to call/email), status, location, timing, who it's assigned to, price (owner/manager), completion note. An "Emails sent to the customer" card lists every email PatchUp has sent for this job — quote, booking confirmation, invoice, follow-ups, payment reminders, review request — with date and time. (Emails go out from PatchUp's address, so they never appear in the tradesperson's own Sent folder — this card is the record.) Buttons: Job notes, Reschedule / Book in, Mark done, and Delete (if no invoice) or Cancel (if an invoice exists).

Job notes

/jobs/notes/[job]

Internal team notes, never shown to the customer (grey banner says so). Type a note, optionally mark it Important (shows amber and surfaces on the complete screen), attach a photo, tap Add. Works offline.

Job photos

/jobs/photos/[job]

A Before / After type dropdown, a camera button, and Before/After galleries. Photos can be deleted with a confirm. Works offline.

09 · Work that repeats

Recurring jobs

For work that repeats — annual services, quarterly checks, monthly maintenance (/jobs/recurring, needs the recurring permission). Set it up once and it books itself.

The list
Blue = active, grey = paused. Each shows "job type · every N weeks/months," the next date, and Edit, Pause/Resume, Delete.
New recurring job
Customer details, job type, location, price, first date, preferred time (or "confirm the time closer to each one"), Repeats every N Weeks/Months/Years, a "let the client know" tick, and who it's assigned to.
What happens
On schedule, PatchUp automatically creates each occurrence as a booked job on the calendar.

10 · The customer book

Clients

/clients (needs the client-database permission). A search box and "+ Add client." Each card shows name, contact, "£X OUTSTANDING" (owner/manager), and a red "Possible duplicate — tap to review" flag when two records look like the same person.

Client detail
Contact card (with Edit), a duplicates section to Merge two records or mark "not a duplicate," and the client's full job history. A client with no jobs can be deleted (owner/manager).
Add / edit
Name (required), Phone, Email, Address.

11 · The money record

Invoices

/invoices (needs the invoice permission) — the full financial record an accountant wants. Search; a custom date range; totals for Total invoiced / Total paid / Outstanding; and download tools — pick a month (or "All invoices") and a format (PDF or CSV for QuickBooks) and Download. Each invoice detail has a payment link editor and Download PDF / CSV.

12 · Your people

Team & permissions

/settings/team (owner/manager). Add the people who work for you and control what they see: current team with a role dropdown (Manager / Subcontractor), a Deactivate/Reactivate button, and a Permissions link. The "Add someone new" form takes their name, email, role, and a starter password you share with them.

Subcontractor permissions

/settings/team/[member]

Six on/off switches, all off by default (they start able only to view and note their own assigned jobs). Saving shows a confirmation of exactly what's changing:

Can invoice
Mark jobs done, send invoices, use the Invoices section.
See clients
Browse the whole client database.
Create quotes
Make and send new quotes.
Quick-book
Book new jobs on the spot.
Recurring
Manage recurring jobs.
Reschedule
Change job dates and times.

13 · Your wording

Message templates

/settings/templates (owner/manager). The wording of every automatic email, so it sounds like you. Each has a subject and body with placeholders. Templates: New quote, Invoice, Manual chase, Auto chase 3 / 7 / 14 days, Booking confirmation, Review request, Payment link note. Each saves on its own.

14 · Your business

Business settings

/settings (owner/manager). How your business appears to customers and on invoices. A "logged in as" card with Log out; nav cards to Billing, Team, Message templates and Help; then the form: Business name, Header tagline, Contact email/phone, Accent colour, Currency (£/$/€), Google review link, Payment terms, Bank details, Invoice footer note, and Save settings. Plus a logo uploader and a "send a test review request" button.

VAT registered? Turn on the VAT toggle and enter your VAT number and rate (standard UK 20%), then pick how you type prices: "It's the total the customer pays" (default — homeowner style) or "It's before VAT — add VAT on top for me" (commercial "£500 + VAT" style: type 500 and the quote goes out as 600, with price fields showing the total that will be charged). Either way invoices show a Net / VAT / Total breakdown with your VAT number, quote emails note the price includes VAT, and the accountant CSV gains net/VAT columns. Each invoice keeps the rate it was issued with, so old invoices never change. Not registered? Leave it off — nothing changes.

15 · The plan

Billing & subscription

/billing (owner/manager). Current plan (a monthly total, "£X per month · N people," a status, and when the trial ends); "Set up payment" or "Manage billing" which open Stripe in a new tab (card details never touch PatchUp); and an honest "If you leave" panel — 30 days after cancelling, everything is deleted (billing records kept 6 years for tax) — with a "Download everything" button that hands you one zip of all your data.

16 · You

Your account

/account (everyone). Your personal login details and this device's notifications: Your name, Your email (needs your password to change), Change password, a notifications toggle, and Log out (for a subcontractor this is the only logout button; logging out also clears the offline copy on the phone).

Notifications toggle: "Get a nudge before a job starts…set per device." On iPhone it first asks you to install to the home screen; on a supported browser it shows "Turn on notifications," then "Turn off" and "Send a test."

17 · No signal, no problem

Working offline — the field view

/field. A special screen that works completely offline because it's saved on the phone — for vans, basements and blackspots.

The banner
Green "You're online — this is your saved copy" or black "No connection — showing your saved day," plus when it was last saved.
Waiting to send
Anything you did offline (completed jobs, notes, photos) queued up, each "sending…" or "when signal returns," with Try again / Discard if needed.
Your next 7 days
Each job with time, customer, tappable phone, location and notes. You can Complete job (amount, note, photos) and Add note here with no signal — it all sends itself when you're back online.

18 · Talk, don't type

Voice & AI features

PatchUp lets you talk instead of type on four screens: New quote, Quick book, Schedule a job, and New reminder.

The voice flow
Tap the mic → it turns red with a pulsing dot and "Stop recording" → tap stop → "Processing recording…" → a grey "Heard: …" box appears with what you said, and the form fields fill themselves in.
The wand
A "Enhance description" / "Enhance note with AI" button tidies typed wording into a clean sentence.
Always optional
Everything voice/AI has a typed equivalent — nothing forces you to use it.

19 · Staying on top of it

Notifications

Two ways PatchUp reaches you: a daily brief email once a day (tomorrow's jobs, invoices due, quotes awaiting a reply — reaches you on any device), and push notifications (if turned on, a nudge about an hour before each job starts; on iPhone only after the app is installed to the home screen).

20 · Ask PatchUp

Getting help

An in-app assistant that instantly answers questions about how to use PatchUp — at Settings → Help (owner/manager) or My account → Help (everyone). A normal screen, not a pop-up.

On screen
A simple chat. Type a question ("How do I send a quote?") or tap an example, and get a short, plain-English answer.
What it does
Explains how features work, grounded only on this guide — it won't make things up.
Hands off
For billing, account issues, or anything not working, it points you to the PatchUp team at hello@getpatchup.co.uk.
